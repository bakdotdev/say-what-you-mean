The kettle had been on since before six, long enough that it was more of a habit than anything, and I did not bother turning it off after the dishes were done. It just kept ticking, some quiet noise folded into the radio and the rain again, and I let it fill up the kitchen while I wiped down the counters and put the last of the shopping away. There is something about that sound that makes a house feel less empty, even when nothing in it is especially interesting. I was not really listening, just letting it move around me while I tidied up. After the sink was clean I went and sat in the front room, the one with the good chair, and turned on the lamp beside it because the light outside had gone that flat grey it does before it starts raining properly. The window in there is older, a little loose depending on the weather, and tonight it kept rattling in and out on one hinge until I got up and switched the latch across. Then it was quiet again, or as quiet as it gets with the road so close.
